# Viability of formalizing the minimal-transvection-decomposition reduction lemmas in Lean 4

**Date:** 2026-08-03 · **Repo:** microsoft/qdk-ec · **Branch:** `lean-transvection-viability`

## 0. Verdict

**Viable with major effort (~3–4.5 person-months for an experienced Lean/mathlib
formalizer).** No missing *external* mathematics gates the scoped target: mathlib
supplies the F2 linear-algebra substrate and the statement is expressible today. The
cost is a self-contained matrix-algebra development over `ZMod 2`. The draft's two
supposed prerequisites — "Sp generated by transvections" and Botha's F2-congruence
classification — are **not on the critical path** for this target, and the char-2
quadratic-form gap is **avoidable**.

Verdict reached by an adversarial two-model review (Claude Opus 4.8 vs GPT Sol 5.6);
both independently landed on *viable-with-major-effort* and converged on ~3–4.5 months.

## 1. Scope (what "minimality" means here)

Target = the **reduction lemmas** l-TET + l-QEQ of
[`transvection-minimality-correction.md`](transvection-minimality-correction.md) §3:

> For symplectic `F ∈ Sp(2m; F2)` with residue rank `r` and invertible residue core
> `E` (from `R·F̂·Rᵀ = diag(E,0)`, `F̂ = Ω(I+F)`), a **length-`r`** transvection
> decomposition of `F` exists **iff** `E` is congruence-lower-triangularizable over F2.

**Out of scope** (per request): the full length function `ℓ(F) ∈ {r, r+1}` for all `m`
(the note only verifies it computationally to `m=6`), and single-`find_fix_vector`
sufficiency. These need the harder Callan / Spengler–Wolff length theory and are noted
as future work only.

## 2. Method

Grounded, not paper-only: installed Lean 4 v4.32.2 + mathlib (master cache), built
`import Mathlib`, and typechecked probe files that `#check` every required primitive,
state the target theorem, and run a `decide` smoke test. Probe sources:
`the Lean probe files (session workspace)/{Probe,Probe2,Capstone,Verify}.lean`. Two frontier models
then adversarially reviewed the findings and each other.

## 4. What must be built (all absent; all expressible)

The capstone probe typechecks (with `sorry`) the following definitions, confirming
they are expressible on mathlib today: `Ω`, `symForm`, symplectic `transvection`,
`IsSymplectic`, `residueMatrix`, `CongruenceTriangularizable`, transvection
product/length, and the biconditional itself. The proof is the work:

1. **F2 symplectic substrate** — `Ω`, `T_v = I+Ωvᵀv`, and its laws (`T_v ∈ Sp`,
   `T_v²=I`, row-action). Bookkeeping via `ext`/`Finset.sum`, not `decide`.
2. **Residue layer** — `F̂ = Ω(I+F)`, `r = F̂.rank = rank(I+F)`, `Res = rowspace`;
   plus the reverse-direction step `I+F = Ω F̂` needing `Ω²=I` (Ω invertible).
3. **Faithful core extraction** — `R·F̂·Rᵀ = diag(E,0)`, `E ∈ GL(r,2)`, **and
   congruence-invariance of `E`** under choice of `R` (well-posedness). Long pole.
4. **l-TET correspondence** — expand `∏ T_{qᵢV}`, and — the heavy step — turn a
   length-`r` transvection list into an *ordered basis of `Res(F)`* via
   rank/rowspace/independence, giving `V = Q·V₀` and `Q E Qᵀ = B⁻ᵀ` (path-counting
   `B`, unit upper-triangular). Long pole.
5. **l-QEQ** — invertible-triangular-over-F2 ⇒ unit diagonal ⇒ `= B⁻ᵀ`; `B` strict-
   upper nilpotence. Light (1–3 wk).
6. **Assembly** — both directions, `r=0` degenerate case, orientation reconciliation.

## 5. Hard findings (empirically verified, not assumed)

- **char-2 is avoidable.** `Invertible (2 : ZMod 2)` fails to synthesize (build error),
  so `QuadraticMap.associated` is unusable over F2. But the l-TET/l-QEQ argument is
  entirely bilinear/matrix-level — only `x·E·yᵀ` and the diagonal `x·E·xᵀ` appear (the
  Rust `bilinear(core,·,·)` is the sole form used), and "invertible triangular ⇒ unit
  diagonal" is ordinary field reasoning. The quadratic↔bilinear correspondence is never
  needed. *Caveat (both reviewers):* "zero diagonal ⇒ alternating" holds only once
  symmetry of the relevant Gram matrix is derived from `Fᵀ Ω F = Ω`; that symmetry
  derivation is a real sub-obligation but stays matrix-level.
- **`BlockTriangular id` is UPPER-triangular**, not lower (verified: `upper.BlockTriangular id`
  proves, `¬ lower.BlockTriangular id` proves). The capstone comment was backwards; lower
  needs `OrderDual.toDual`, and the orientation must match l-QEQ's `Q E Qᵀ = B⁻ᵀ`
  (B upper unit-diag ⇒ `Q E Qᵀ` lower). Existential upper/lower are equivalent under the
  `Fin r` reversal permutation, but the *ordered* basis in l-QEQ pins the orientation.
- **Sp-generation and Botha are out of scope.** The biconditional's forward direction
  uses only the *given* length-`r` product (via rank ⇒ basis ⇒ `V=Q·V₀`); the backward
  direction starts from the *existential* `Q`. Neither needs "every Sp element decomposes"
  nor a classification of *which* cores triangularize.
- **Capstone faithfulness caveat.** The original capstone's `_hcore : True` decouples `E`
  from `F`, making the displayed biconditional vacuous — "it compiles" alone is not
  evidence of faithful expressibility. A real `R·F̂·Rᵀ=diag(E,0)` hypothesis was
  substituted and **also compiles** (`Verify.lean`), so expressibility holds; the gap
  was the placeholder, not the language.
- **`decide` does not scale.** The passing `Fᵀ Ω F = Ω` smoke test is fixed `n=4` only;
  it gives no evidence about symbolic `Fin r` matrix-product / rank-to-basis ergonomics,
  which is the main schedule risk.

| 3. Faithful core extraction + congruence-invariance of `E` | 3–6 wk |

**Long poles:** faithful/invariant **core extraction** (#3) and the **rank-to-basis**
induction inside l-TET (#2) — *not* the `B`-matrix algebra, Sp-generation, or Botha.

## 7. Recommended path

1. Build on **mathlib only**, over `Matrix (Fin (2m)) (ZMod 2)`. Ignore PhysLean/cslib.
2. Keep the entire argument **bilinear/matrix-level**; never touch `QuadraticMap.associated`.
3. State the theorem **witness-parameterized** (`R, E` satisfying `R·F̂·Rᵀ=diag(E,0)` as
   hypotheses) to sidestep canonical-core well-posedness initially; add congruence-
   invariance of `E` only if a canonical "the residue core" API is later exposed.
4. Fix orientation up front (lower-triangular = `OrderDual.toDual`) to match `B⁻ᵀ`.
5. Land the substrate + `decide`-checked small cases first, then the symbolic inductions.

## 8. Residual disagreements (surfaced by the adversarial review)

- **Weight of the `B`-induction:** initially rated a top long pole; reconciled to light
  (1–3 wk), with the heavy induction relabeled as the rank-to-basis step. Agreed.
- **Effort band:** Sol 2–4 mo vs Opus 3–5 mo → converged to ≈3–4.5 mo; the upper end is
  reserved for `Fin r` rewrite friction, which the probe could not measure.
- **Biggest risk item:** "symbolic matrix-product ergonomics" (Opus) vs "block/reindex +
  rank-to-basis reasoning" (Sol) — unresolved; both are `Fin`-indexing pain, only a real
  formalization attempt on component #2 would settle which dominates.
